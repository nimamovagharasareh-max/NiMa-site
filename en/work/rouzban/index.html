Rouzban, your everyday companion

A Persian app for everyday life: tasks with the Persian (Solar Hijri) calendar, money and the people you lend to or borrow from, health and progress. It installs on the phone, works offline and sends reminders right on time.

TypeInstallable web app (PWA)
DevicesiPhone, Android and desktop
PriceFree
DataStays on the user's own phone

Use Rouzban ↗

Why we built it

Daily tasks, spending and money lent to friends usually end up scattered across several apps, notebooks and memory. We wanted one app that is Persian and right-to-left, gets the Persian calendar right, understands plain language, and keeps each person's data on their own phone. That is Rouzban.

What we built

  • Plain-language entry: type "350 lunch", "lent Ali 6 million" or "tomorrow 5 pm meeting" in Persian; it works out the amount, transaction type and time, and shows them before saving.
  • Jibban (money): accounts, spending and income, transfers between accounts, and people with what they owe you, what you owe them and the full history. No account balance can go negative.
  • Tasks and Persian calendar: an accurate calendar including leap years, a time for each task, and push-notification reminders right on time.
  • Health and progress: water, sleep, weight and exercise, with weekly and monthly trend charts.
  • Feels like a real app: installs on the home screen without an app store, works offline, and follows the phone's light or dark mode.
  • Privacy: data stays on the user's phone and moves with a backup file. Only a task's title and time are sent to the server, and only if the user turns reminders on.

Outcome

Rouzban is hosted on a server inside Iran, so it loads fast without filtering, and anyone can install and use it for free.
