Payaco company website

A trilingual website for an elevator and escalator company with over two decades of experience in Ahvaz, built to present its services and projects and guide visitors to request a consultation and site visit.

ClientPayaco Elevator Co., Ahvaz
IndustryElevators & escalators
ServicesWebsite design & development
LanguagesPersian, Arabic, English

Visit the live site ↗

The challenge

Anyone looking for an elevator, whether a developer or a building manager, wants to be sure they are dealing with an experienced technical team before they call. Payaco needed a website that clearly shows its services and real projects, in three languages.

What we built

  • Services pages: design and engineering, installation and commissioning, service and maintenance, repairs and troubleshooting, and technical consulting, each clearly explained.
  • Completed projects: finished work with photos, location, number of elevators and stops, and year of handover.
  • Online cabin design tool: takes the shaft dimensions, number of stops and door positions, and shows a proposed layout with plan, elevations, a 3D view and a printable summary.
  • Quick contact: phone, Instagram and a consultation and site-visit request page, reachable from every page.
  • Three languages: Persian, Arabic and English; light and fast, on the company's own server.

The result

Payaco now has a professional online showcase in three languages that presents its services and projects and leads customers to request a consultation and site visit.

For Payaco's internal work we also built the comprehensive office automation system.
