Payaco

A trilingual website to present the company, and a web app to run service, repairs and finance, for a company that installs and maintains elevators and escalators.

ClientPayaco Elevator Co., Ahvaz
IndustryElevators & escalators
ServicesWebsite + automation
LanguagesPersian, Arabic, English

The challenge

The daily work of an elevator service company, from scheduled maintenance and repairs to finance follow-ups, usually lives across paper, messaging apps and scattered files. Payaco needed both a professional website to present itself and a single system for its internal work.

What we built

  • Company website (payaco.org): a light, fast site in Persian, Arabic and English, hosted on the company's own server.
  • Service, repairs & finance app (app.payaco.org): an installable web app (PWA) that brings service, repair and finance work into one place.
  • Ongoing development: the system keeps improving release by release and is in daily use.

The result

Payaco now has a professional online presence in three languages, and its internal work runs in one system instead of many scattered places.
